## The Pain Points (Ranked by Severity)

### Pain 1: Inconsistent Content Quality
"We'll get one great video and three mediocre ones. There's no system — it depends on which editor is free that week."

**How Perokio solves it:** Every video follows a B11-verified script. The brand pre-approves the claim, the sources, and the tone. The creator films exactly what's written. Quality is consistent by design.

### Pain 2: Zero ROI Tracking
"I know we spent $3,000 last month on 'brand awareness' content. I have no idea if it drove any orders. My CEO asks me every week and I change the subject."

**How Perokio solves it:** Every video has a `pero.to/` smart link → tracks clicks → links to a conversion page → tracks orders via TastyIgniter webhooks → shows Revenue in the B08 Command Center. **The brand sees exact dollars, not vanity metrics.**

### Pain 3: Fear of AI Hallucinations
"We tried ChatGPT to write our scripts. It said our coffee 'boosts metabolism' and 'is rich in antioxidants.' Our lawyer nearly had a heart attack."

**How Perokio solves it:** The B11 Gate physically blocks publishing if any script line lacks a verified source. The AI can suggest, but it cannot publish. A human QA reviewer confirms every line. SHA-256 receipt proves the verification happened.

### Pain 4: Creator Content is Off-Script
"We paid an influencer $500 for a sponsored post. She improvised the entire thing, mentioned our competitor, and the caption said 'not sponsored.' Never again."

**How Perokio solves it:** Creators claim a Fact Bundle → film the locked script → the brand reviews the video with a 12h timer → approves or requests changes. The transcript is compared against the script using Jaccard similarity. Deviation > 30% → `held_factual_deviation`.

### Pain 5: Slow Turnaround
"By the time our agency delivers the Ramadan campaign, Ramadan is over."

**How Perokio solves it:** The Weekly Promo Cycle. Monday 9am: next week locks. AI generates the script in seconds, QA verifies in minutes, creators film within 24h, brand approves within 12h. **From idea to published video: 48 hours, not 2 weeks.**
