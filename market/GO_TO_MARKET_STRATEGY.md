## Competitive GTM Positioning

### vs. Traditional Agencies (Primary competitor in Tunisia)

**Their pitch:** "We'll create beautiful content for you."
**Our pitch:** "We'll create verified content AND show you exactly how many orders it drove. If we don't drive 20 orders in 14 days, you don't pay."

**Kill shot:** Show the brand their current agency invoice ($3,000/mo) next to the Perokio dashboard showing $1,350 in tracked orders from 4 videos. The math is undeniable.

### vs. Billo / Insense (UGC platforms)

**Their pitch:** "We'll connect you with creators who make UGC videos."
**Our pitch:** "We'll connect you with creators who make VERIFIED videos with locked scripts, tracked through to actual orders via your TastyIgniter POS."

**Kill shot:** Billo videos have no source verification. One hallucinated claim = lawsuit. Perokio's B11 engine makes hallucination physically impossible.

### vs. Jasper / Copy.ai

**Their pitch:** "AI writes your marketing copy."
**Our pitch:** "AI writes your marketing copy, but only from verified sources. Then it distributes the content to real creators. Then it tracks the orders. Then it tells you what to do next week."

**Kill shot:** Jasper writes text. Perokio runs the entire revenue loop — from ideation to order tracking.
