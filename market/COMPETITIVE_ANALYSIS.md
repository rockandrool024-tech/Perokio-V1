## Deep-Dive: Perokio vs. Each Competitor

### 1. vs. Traditional Agencies

**Who they are:** Local Tunisian/Gulf marketing agencies that produce social media content for F&B brands. Examples: Matchbox, H Mediagency, L&P.

**Their strengths:**
- Established relationships with brand owners
- Full-service (photography, design, strategy, media buying)
- Local market knowledge

**Their weaknesses:**
- **Expensive:** $2,000-$10,000/mo for 4-8 pieces of content
- **Slow:** 2-3 week turnaround per campaign
- **Zero ROI tracking:** They deliver "brand awareness" content with no order attribution
- **Inconsistent quality:** Depends on which editor/designer is assigned
- **No AI leverage:** Purely manual, doesn't scale

**How Perokio wins:**
- 6-20x cheaper ($499 vs $2,000-$10,000)
- 10x faster (48 hours from idea to published video vs 2-3 weeks)
- Full ROI tracking (Revenue attribution via TastyIgniter)
- B11-verified scripts (consistent quality, zero hallucination risk)
- AI-powered (RAG pipeline generates scripts in seconds, not days)

**Positioning statement:** "Your agency charges $3,000/mo for 4 videos you can't track. Perokio charges $499/mo for 60 verified content pieces AND shows you exactly how many orders they drove."

### 2. vs. Billo / Insense (UGC Platforms)

**Who they are:** Global UGC (User-Generated Content) platforms that connect brands with creators who produce authentic-looking video content. Billo raised $10M+, Insense is YC-backed.

**Their strengths:**
- Large creator networks (thousands of vetted creators)
- Authentic content style (native to TikTok/Instagram)
- Self-serve platform
- Global reach

**Their weaknesses:**
- **No script verification:** Creators improvise. No source-checking. Legal risk for the brand.
- **No order tracking:** They track views and engagement, not orders or revenue
- **No landing pages:** They don't build conversion pages or smart links
- **No AI integration:** No content generation, no optimization
- **No MENA focus:** US/EU-first. Limited Arabic/French support. No TND payouts.
- **Generic content:** "Make a video about our product" — no verified facts, no locked scripts

**How Perokio wins:**
- B11 Engine: every script line is source-verified before a creator can film it
- TastyIgniter integration: we track ORDERS, not just views
- Campaign Studio: we build the conversion page + smart link + revenue funnel
- AI Copilot: we tell the brand what to do next week based on actual ROI data
- MENA-native: Arabic + French i18n, Flouci payouts in TND, Tunisia-first GTM
- Weekly Promo Cycle: brands operate on a system, not ad-hoc campaigns

**Positioning statement:** "Billo gives you creator videos. Perokio gives you verified creator videos that are tracked through to actual orders in your POS."

### 3. vs. Jasper / Copy.ai (AI Copywriting)

**Who they are:** AI-powered copywriting tools that generate marketing text. Jasper raised $125M at a $1.5B valuation. Copy.ai raised $11M+.

**Their strengths:**
- Fast content generation (seconds, not hours)
- Cheap ($99/mo for Jasper, free tier for Copy.ai)
- Broad use cases (emails, ads, blogs, social)
- Well-funded with large user bases

**Their weaknesses:**
- **Hallucinate freely:** No source verification. One false claim = legal exposure for the brand.
- **Text only:** No video, no creators, no distribution
- **No tracking:** They write copy but can't tell you if it drove any orders
- **No MENA focus:** English-first, limited Arabic support
- **Generic output:** No brand voice, no audience context, no industry specialization

**How Perokio wins:**
- B11 Engine: AI can SUGGEST but cannot PUBLISH. Every line is verified against official sources.
- Full-stack: We generate the script → verify it → distribute to creators → track the orders
- RAG pipeline: Our AI knows the brand's voice, audience, and verified sources
- TastyIgniter: We connect the content to actual revenue
- MENA-native: Arabic + Derja + French content generation

**Positioning statement:** "Jasper writes text that might be true. Perokio writes text that IS true — verified against official sources — then distributes it to real creators and tracks the orders it drives."

### 4. vs. HubSpot / Salesforce (Marketing Automation)

**Who they are:** Enterprise B2B SaaS platforms for marketing, sales, and CRM. HubSpot: $1.7B revenue. Salesforce: $31B revenue.

**Their strengths:**
- Massive feature set (CRM, email, automation, analytics, ads)
- Enterprise-grade integrations
- Large ecosystems
- Strong brand recognition

**Their weaknesses:**
- **Not built for F&B:** Generic B2B SaaS. No creator marketplace, no script verification, no content production
- **Expensive:** $800-$3,600/mo for the features a QSR brand needs
- **Complex:** Requires a dedicated admin to set up and maintain
- **No content creation:** They track marketing but don't produce it
- **No MENA focus:** US-first, English-only, USD pricing
- **Overkill for a 5-location restaurant chain**

**How Perokio wins:**
- Built specifically for F&B creator economy (script verification + creator marketplace + order tracking)
- 60x cheaper than a full HubSpot stack ($499 vs $3,000+/mo)
- Simple: a brand can go from signup to first published video in 60 minutes
- TastyIgniter integration is native (not a custom API project)
- The AI Copilot is trained on F&B ROI patterns, not generic marketing data

**Positioning statement:** "HubSpot is a marketing platform for B2B SaaS companies. Perokio is a Revenue OS for restaurant chains. We produce the content, distribute it to creators, and track the orders. HubSpot can't do any of that."
